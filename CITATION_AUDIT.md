# SIS conjectures: independent prior-art and claim audit

Audit date: 20 October 2026. Prepared by the independent matrix/control scout and supplemented by the SIS/probability scout after the Hölder extension. This is a bounded literature audit, not a claim that no unindexed or unpublished antecedent exists.

## 1. Target and object identity

The target is the deterministic, community-resolved SIS system

\[
z_i'=(1-z_i)\sum_jw_{ij}\pi_jz_j-\gamma_i z_i,
\qquad W=W^T,\quad \Pi=\operatorname{diag}(\pi),\quad A=W\Pi.
\]

The complete observed object is the vector trajectory, rather than the aggregate prevalence alone. The reconstruction object is a symmetric contact matrix W with known initial state, recovery rates and population proportions. Two candidate contact matrices produce the same observed trajectory when their difference annihilates \(\Pi z(t)\) for every t.

The parameter box in the target paper fixes m and M independently of dimension, with initial components in [m,1-m], recoveries in [0,M], contact entries in [m,M], nonnegative population fractions summing to one. Reconstruction statements assume strictly positive population fractions. Prediction statements permit zero fractions.

The precise publication is:

Keliger, D., & Horváth, I. (2026). Why is it easier to predict the epidemic curve than to reconstruct the underlying contact network? *Journal of Mathematical Biology, 92*, Article 82. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00285-026-02401-6

Primary full text: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13135567/ . Preprint full text: https://arxiv.org/html/2506.00059v2 . Publication metadata inspected: published 2 May 2026; received 28 August 2025; revised 16 February 2026; accepted 6 April 2026.

Conjecture 3.3 asserts that reconstructibility is equivalent to the absence of a nontrivial block-regular partition. Such a partition requires equal initial values and equal recovery rates within each block, in addition to equality of aggregate contact weights into every block.

Conjecture 3.11 has two distinct claims. Its equation (14) asks for a finite interval-norm amplification bound at each fixed dimension, uniformly over the parameter box. Equation (15) asks for a bound uniform also in dimension. The seminorm is generated by

\[
G(T)=\int_0^Tz(t)z(t)^T\,dt.
\]

The amplification bound concerns the neighbourhood-statistic residual \((A-\widehat A)z(t)\), evaluated on the true trajectory. It is not, by itself, a lower bound for discrepancy between the two nonlinear solution trajectories. The original Theorem 3.12 proves a weaker, dimension-free absolute uniform-continuity statement for bounded contact matrices. An unbounded relative/Lipschitz factor does not contradict that theorem.

## 3. Inspected sources and relevance

2. **Prasse, B., Devriendt, K., & Van Mieghem, P. (2021).** Clustering for epidemics on networks: A geometric approach. *Chaos, 31*(6), Article 063115. https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0048779 . Their notation maps as \(B=W\Pi\), \(\delta_i=\gamma_i\); for uniform populations the symmetric-matrix conventions agree. Their Theorem 2 is a related invariant-subspace classification under the additional requirement that the recovery diagonal matrix preserves the subspace. That assumption must not silently be imposed on heterogeneous invariant rays.

3. **Novikov, D., & Yakovenko, S. ## 4. Positive collision: complete-network homogeneous-recovery solution

The proposed readable example uses \(n=3\), uniform populations, \(W=\mathbf1\mathbf1^T\), homogeneous recovery \(\gamma=1/2\), and pairwise different initial coordinates. The exact trajectory belongs to \(\operatorname{span}\{\mathbf1,z(0)\}\); hence a nonzero vector orthogonal to this plane gives a rank-one symmetric matrix perturbation preserving the trajectory. Pairwise different initial coordinates rule out every proper block-regular partition.

The low-dimensional complete-network solution is precisely the content of Prasse–Devriendt–Van Mieghem Theorem 4, not a new dynamical reduction. Applying it to contradict the later block-regularity conjecture is a valid source correction. The appropriate wording is that the conjecture is already contradicted by this explicit consequence of the 2021 theorem. The short proof may be reproduced for reader convenience with clear attribution.

### Positive collision: generic Hölder continuation

The simplification to bounded holomorphic functions triggers a separate novelty gate. Hölder dependence on data in a smaller set, and its continuation along overlapping discs, are established principles; Trefethen (2020) provides a direct primary comparison. They cannot be counted as new general theorems here.

The retained SIS claim is more specific: bounded coefficients and population normalisation give a complex-time radius \(1/(12M)\) and trajectory sup bound 2 independently of dimension. Normalising the residual by \(2M\), or the difference of two trajectories by 4, gives the same analytic and real Lipschitz bounds. An explicit interpolation proof then gives the stated \(L^2\)-to-\(L^2\) SIS modulus with constants depending only on \(M,T_0,T\). No optimality is claimed. The direct trajectory extension permits different contact matrices, recovery vectors and initial states, with common communities and population weights, and uses trajectory observations without differentiation.

This is an explicit uniform application, with modest methodological novelty, not evidence for a new analytic-continuation principle. Conversely, instability of unrestricted bounded analytic functions would not by itself construct an admissible positive SIS obstruction. The complete-network/binomial family supplies that separate model-specific step.

## 6. Independent mathematical checks and interpretation

The manuscript's two-community heterogeneous example has \(W_0=(2/3)\mathbf1\mathbf1^T\), \(W_1=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}1&1/2\\1/2&3/4\end{smallmatrix}\right)\), \(\pi=(1/2,1/2)\), \(\gamma=(3/4,1/4)\), and \(z=x(1,2)^T\), where \(x'=x/4-x^2\) and \(x(0)=1/8\). The difference is \(vv^T/12\) for \(v=(2,-1)^T\), which annihilates \(\Pi(1,2)^T\). Distinct initial coordinates and recoveries preclude block regularity. Both matrices lie in the source's fixed positive box. These are exact algebraic statements irrespective of their eventual novelty status.

For the dimension family, index communities by i=0,...,d, take n=d+1, all contact entries one, uniform populations, \(\gamma_i=i/d\), and common initial value \(c_0=1/2\). With \(r=\frac1n\sum_i z_i\), \(H(t)=\int_0^t r\), and \(v_i=(-1)^i\binom di\), the exact variation-of-constants formula yields

\[
v^Tz(t)=e^{-H(t)}\left[c_0 f_d(t)+\int_0^t r(s)e^{H(s)}f_d(t-s)\,ds\right],
\qquad f_d(t)=(1-e^{-t/d})^d.
\]

Thus \(c_0e^{-t}f_d(t)\le v^Tz(t)\le f_d(t)\). For \(T_0<s<T\), its ratio of observation norms is at least

\[
c_0e^{-(T+s)}\sqrt{\frac{T-s}{T_0}}\left(\frac{s}{T_0}\right)^d.
\]

The construction has a positive-definite G(T0), not merely a nullspace artefact: if another coefficient vector annihilates z identically, the variation-of-constants formula gives a homogeneous Volterra equation for its distinct-exponential sum. Uniqueness forces that sum and then every coefficient to vanish.

The displayed binomial direction has the exact exponential rate \(\lim R_d^{1/d}=T/T_0\); only \(\liminf\Lambda_d^{1/d}\geq T/T_0\) is asserted for the optimal continuation factor. A reverse use of the same positive bounds proves the former, so no numerical asymptotic inference is needed.

The Hölder audit checks the complex Picard bounds, the \(L^2\)-to-sup exponent \(2/3\), the Lagrange interpolation factor \((4e)^N\), the Cauchy remainder, and iteration of actual suprema bounded by one. For the direct trajectory extension, complex norm at most 4 and real derivative norm at most \(4M\) give the same normalised constants. These calculations establish an explicit valid estimate, not optimality or practical sharpness.

The contribution package is consequently a rigorous resolution of the two quantifiers in Conjecture 3.11, an explicit dimension-free SIS Hölder replacement and direct-observation extension, together with a correction of Conjecture 3.3 and the heterogeneous classification. Its mathematical value should not be assessed by counting these as independent new general principles. The novelty language remains deliberately bounded because the generic continuation machinery and complete-network reduction are prior art. See `CLAIM_MAP.md` for the claim ledger and dependencies.
