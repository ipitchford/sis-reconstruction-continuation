# Primary sources

The paper's bibliography and `CITATION_AUDIT.md` give complete details. The principal source question is:

- D. Keliger and I. Horvath, “Why is it easier to predict the epidemic curve than to reconstruct the underlying contact network?”, *Journal of Mathematical Biology* 92 (2026), Article 82, <https://doi.org/10.1007/s00285-026-02401-6>.

The fixed-dimensional proof uses established polynomial-ideal closure associated with Novikov and Yakovenko. The Hölder continuation route is explicitly compared with Trefethen's quantitative analytic-continuation results. The complete-network reduction is credited to Prasse, Devriendt and Van Mieghem.
